About Me

Who I Am

Tegha Treasure Ikei Ringket

I'm a passionate software developer based in Yaoundé, Cameroon, with a strong focus on building practical, impactful digital products. From AI-powered browser extensions to mobile apps and full-stack web platforms, I love turning ideas into working software.

My experience spans frontend and backend development, mobile (Android with Kotlin & Flutter/Dart), and cybersecurity tooling. I built Verai — an AI-powered Chrome extension for phishing detection — which reflects my deep interest in using technology to keep people safe online.

I'm an AI enthusiast too: I built Ask Jo, a bilingual English/French AI counsellor for young people in Cameroon and across Africa, and I'm certified in Introduction to Modern AI by Cisco Networking Academy.

I'm also Preply-certified in language teaching, with a passion for helping learners build fluency and confidence in conversational English. Explaining ideas clearly, whether code, security concepts, or grammar, is something I genuinely enjoy.

I thrive in collaborative environments and have worked with teams on projects like ConnectSphere, a Facebook-inspired social platform. I'm always learning, always building, and always looking for ways to make technology serve real human needs.
